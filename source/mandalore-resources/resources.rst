Resources
=========

.. _mandalore-resources:

Mandalore's "offline resources" are a neat choice of standalone tools and scripts that are often used during penetration tests,
CTFs and red-teams.
While many penetration testers download those resources again every time they need them, Mandalore users don't have to.
Everything is managed by the wrapper and they are shared with every container by default (at ``/opt/resources``).
